Outcome of the 0.5 release work (agreed in the 2026-10-30 session, finished 2026-11-05): release 0.5.0 is PUBLISHED (tag v0.5.0, https://github.com/ninja-96/tinker-edge-r-debian/releases/tag/v0.5.0). Linux 6.18.60, Debian 13.7, the LiteRT 2.3.2 NPU stack, test-board.sh RESULT: PASS (49 passed, 7 skipped, 0 failed). The full procedure is in mem:project/release-procedure.

What was checked on the board (2026-11-05): the full test-board.sh on the fresh image (network 444/586 Mbit/s, NPU selftest 17/17, eMMC ~164 MiB/s read, max 50 C); the NPU with a model loaded survives the suspend ENTER (models stay loaded), but SUSPEND IS NOT USABLE: the RTC alarm does not wake the board (rtcwake -m mem -s 90 with -s, NOT -l +90; the board stayed asleep until the user pressed reset; the RTC itself keeps the time; journal of the sleep ends at the rtcwake moment with no PM resume). README + ROADMAP + the 0.5 notes say: keep the board powered on. Next: find why the rk808 alarm does not raise the wake interrupt (dtsi/pmic wake path or the driver), then retest suspend/resume with a model and a USB keyboard.

What the maintainer did not want in 0.5 (skipped by decision): Wi-Fi/BT connection tests with the antennas, HDMI audio by ear, the boot logo on the screen, USB 3 over USB-C, SD boot, KDE, the 40-pin electrical side.
